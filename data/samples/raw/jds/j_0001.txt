Senior Backend Engineer (Node.js & Cloud Infrastructure)

About the Role:
We are seeking an experienced Senior Backend Engineer to design and scale our next-generation distributed backend platform. You will lead the architecture of high-throughput microservices, optimize database performance, and deploy scalable containerized services to AWS.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design, build, and maintain mission-critical backend services in Node.js and TypeScript.
- Architect high-performance PostgreSQL databases, indexing strategies, and caching solutions with Redis.
- Orchestrate and manage containerized microservices using Docker and Kubernetes in AWS.
- Collaborate with frontend engineers and product teams to define resilient REST and GraphQL APIs.

Requirements:
- 5+ years of software engineering experience focusing on backend and distributed systems.
- Deep expertise in Node.js, TypeScript, and modern JavaScript runtime environments.
- Strong proficiency with PostgreSQL, schema design, query optimization, and connection pooling.
- Hands-on experience with Docker, Kubernetes, and AWS cloud services (ECS, RDS, S3).
- Solid understanding of asynchronous patterns, message queues (Kafka/RabbitMQ), and microservice resilience.
