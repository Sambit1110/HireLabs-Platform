Machine Learning Engineer - Semantic Search & NLP

About the Role:
Join our AI platform team to build state-of-the-art semantic search, neural retrieval, and dense embedding systems. You will fine-tune contrastive embedding models, optimize vector retrieval with pgvector/HNSW, and deploy production inference microservices.

Key Responsibilities:
- Fine-tune dense retrieval and sentence embedding models using PyTorch and Hugging Face / Sentence-Transformers.
- Design and benchmark vector retrieval pipelines with pgvector, HNSW indexes, and hybrid BM25 search.
- Package and serve low-latency inference models using FastAPI, Docker, and ONNX Runtime.
- Evaluate information retrieval metrics: Recall@K, MRR, and NDCG on domain-specific datasets.

Requirements:
- 3+ years of experience developing and deploying machine learning or NLP models.
- Deep knowledge of Transformer architectures, contrastive learning (MultipleNegativesRankingLoss), and dense representations.
- Proficient in Python, PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers, and Sentence-Transformers.
- Experience with vector databases, pgvector, similarity search metrics (cosine, dot product), and embedding serving.
